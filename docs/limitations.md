# Scope and remaining limits

## Current core evaluation

The current implementation, resource policy and statistical definitions are in [core_rebuild/README.md](../experiments/core_rebuild/README.md). The new evidence is limited to 28 conditions at `N=1024` on one WSL2 host; it supplies no measured scaling in `N`, network transport, cross-host prediction or comparison with external PIR systems. Four guest CPU positions do not establish physical host exclusivity, and the 8 GiB sampled monitor policy is not a hard limit or an exact memory peak.

Same-width and independently tuned repeated retrieval remain distinct contrasts. Packed retrieval is not uniformly faster: the independently tuned primary comparison at `alpha=4, ell=32768` with preprocessing included has an interval spanning one, and the full-width repeated baseline is faster in the two included-scope `alpha=2` capacity conditions beyond the packed block boundary. Lower measured CPU cost does not imply lower preprocessing cost or lower latency. Actual layout-dependent reply counts, all ten session effects and pointwise uncertainty are public in the complete summary/CSV; ciphertext buffers are not wire traffic or peak memory. Successive sessions show time variation and do not establish independent-day replication, stationarity or interval coverage. Finite validation adds no security premise or native failure-probability guarantee.
