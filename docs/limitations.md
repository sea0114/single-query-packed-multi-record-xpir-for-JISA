# Scope and remaining limits

- The formal rounded Gaussian law, symbolic noise width, efficient-sampling premise and distribution-specific PLWE assumption are not a native security certificate. Native bounded biased sampling and OS-seeded randomness have no completed joint-law/computational bridge here.
- Conditional no-wrap recovery and the Nn common-event bound do not establish an unconditional native failure probability. Privacy uses one fresh key and one challenge, N+1 samples and explicit unit-probability loss; no SPIR, CCA, adaptive key reuse or decryption oracle is added.
- The measured comparator is concurrent same-profile repeated XPIR with private preprocessing/state. There is no completed optimized cross-profile XPIR or external-PIR timing comparison.
- One WSL2 host, host-managed scheduling/frequency and differing resource bundles limit generalization. Multiplicity changes worker CPU count and width; it is not fixed-total-core scaling.
- Historical bootstrap intervals are pointwise. Three successive later sessions give descriptive ranges, not independent-day confidence intervals. The primary/multiplicity two-record difference remains unexplained and is not pooled away.
- Historical monitor traces are unavailable. Later sampled traces are archived, but do not prove instantaneous limits or zero monitor overhead. Summed worker lifetime RSS is not synchronized peak memory.
- Ciphertext buffers are not measured transport traffic. The flat packed query exceeds raw database size at every reported length. No raw-download superiority or benchmark of a coefficient-position alternative is asserted.
- The source fetch/build route preserves upstream notices and records new identities. Some old upstream files lack specific notices; they are obtained separately from the official source and are not redistributed or relabeled as project-owned code.
- JISA-specific current formatting rules were not retrievable during revision. Funding, competing-interest and author-contribution statements are unfilled by author instruction. AI-use and final author approval declarations await actual author review. This is not a ready-to-submit certification.
