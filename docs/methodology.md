# Measurement and statistical definitions

The paper reports four experiments separately. The original names remain inside immutable evidence; their one-to-one mapping is in `experiment-map.md`. All measurements predate this release. Packaging performed replay and functional validation only.

Each task retrieves an ordered tuple of distinct record indices. Packed and concurrent repeated workers use the same public encoding/profile within a comparison, fresh client keys and private database preprocessing. A repeated task is measured as latest worker end minus earliest worker start. No artificial network round trip is added. Cold includes encoding/import/server setup; online stages private database state but still includes fresh client configuration/key setup, query, reply and extraction. Process startup, fixture generation, teardown, logging and post-endpoint validation are excluded. No transport is measured.

The primary design has 96 cells: alpha=2, N=256/1024/4096/16384, record length=256/512/1024/2048 bits, segment width=8/12/16, and both views. Per cell: three warmup pairs and 30/30/20/10 measured pairs by increasing N. There are 2,160 measured pairs. The packed worker is serial with a two-logical-CPU affinity allowance; two repeated workers are pinned separately. The parent has the same allowance. Packed address space is capped at 16 GiB, each repeated worker at 8 GiB, aggregate sampled RSS at 16 GiB.

The multiplicity design has 24 cells: alpha=2/3/4, N=1024/4096, record length=512/2048, segment width=8, coefficient width=8alpha, and both views. Ten measured and three warmup pairs per cell. Packed is pinned to one CPU; alpha repeated workers occupy distinct CPUs in a four-CPU parent allowance. Each worker has an 8 GiB address-space limit, with a sampled 16 GiB aggregate virtual-memory limit. This is not fixed-total-core scaling. The two-record row is an internal reference; it does not replace or combine with the primary design.

The archived estimator is the median of paired repeated/packed task-time ratios. Primary and multiplicity intervals are 10,000-resample pointwise percentile bootstrap intervals, using cell-reset seeds 2026092401 and 2026092501. Separate medians are checked independently. Schedule balance and exact deterministic replay do not establish iid data, coverage under dependence or simultaneous coverage. No independent historical sessions are asserted. Failed/incomplete attempts remain recorded; no retries, outlier deletion or imputation is applied.

The later sensitivity/capacity study fixes three successive sessions, each with three warmups and ten measured pairs per cell and balanced five/five method order. Cells are interleaved and do not run concurrently. Missing or failed warmups withhold that cell/session's estimate. Pilot data are excluded. Protocol stopping rules are resource/correctness/integrity based, not effect based. The eight sensitivity and ten capacity cells have 540 measured and 162 warmup pairs. All completed. Ranges are observed minima/maxima of three session medians, not confidence intervals or independent-day replication.

Sensitivity at N=1024 and 512-bit records crosses record-field/full-block extraction/API bundles with two-CPU/four-CPU-parent resource bundles. These bundle comparisons do not isolate one cause of the primary/multiplicity difference. Capacity fixes N=1024, alpha=4, width=32 and full-block/four-CPU-parent execution, at lengths 4096/8192/12288/16384/65536. The last uses two polynomial blocks. The coefficient-slot boundary alpha J/n=1 differs from the record-block boundary J=n; no alternative position-packing protocol was benchmarked.

CPU sums worker work, not critical-path time. Summed worker lifetime RSS peaks are not synchronized memory usage. Historical endpoint records cannot reconstruct missing historical monitoring samples. Later monitoring records actual timestamps at nominal 10 ms; neither zero monitoring overhead nor instantaneous maxima is established.

Codex (OpenAI; GPT-6-based assistant, exact deployment version unavailable) assisted manuscript restructuring and new packaging/presentation/validation code. Frozen observations, estimators and hashes were preserved and numerically checked.
